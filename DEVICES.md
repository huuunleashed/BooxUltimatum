# Tablets and what works on them

This page says what is known about BooxUltimatum and Nib on each tablet, feature by feature. The maintainer checks everything on a Note Air6 C. The other columns come from owners of other tablets, mostly through GitHub issues, and say only what they reported. A blank cell means nobody has reported on that feature there yet, not that it fails.

The same table is on the website, at [huuunleashed.github.io/BooxUltimatum/#tablets](https://huuunleashed.github.io/BooxUltimatum/#tablets). The *Access* column is the least access a feature needs, as [How much access it needs](README.md#how-much-access-it-needs) explains: T0 is the app alone, T1 the one-time `adb` grants, and T2 Shizuku.

### Notes

1. Simplified Chinese seen on the tablet, drawn in its own system font, which has Chinese (issue #6).
2. Every page read in Chinese on a tablet set to Chinese, with the translation added to 0.11.0, before the Language row existed. The Language row's route for Android 11 and 12 isn't checked yet.
3. The Language row and Android's own App languages screen, in Simplified Chinese and back.
4. In use when its owner reported that Boox-frozen apps were missing from it (issue #3).
5. On the emulator, and on a Bigme B7 by its owner's report.
6. Built in 0.7.0 from its owner's report (issue #3). Not confirmed on that tablet since.
7. Its owner was asked to try it in issue #5, as the way round the missing sticker album. No answer yet.
8. Checked on battery. Measured there, the one-minute step cost about 1.6 mAh an hour, so it now runs only while charging.
9. Built from Boox's own code and checked against renders on the emulator. Not yet seen through a real sleep.
10. Boox's settings on this tablet have no sticker album, so the plate has nowhere to go (issue #5).
11. Boox saves its copy of the picture, per its own log. The screen at a real power-off isn't looked at yet.
12. Its owner reports that the power-off screen works (issue #5).
13. With a real pen in Sketchbook.
14. No effect on 0.4.1. Its owner reported it working once 0.5 found the pen by how it behaves and checked usage access (issues #1 and #2).
15. Its owner uses it for handwriting, through the direct display route (issue #6).
16. Without the display's preview: Nib draws every stroke itself.
17. With a battery log copied from a Note Air6 C.
18. Android 16 asks for one more grant, READ_DROPBOX_DATA, to read where a restart is recorded.
19. Switch off a display that stays on, and Refresh the sleep screen while asleep, are on by default since 0.10.1. Neither has been seen to work yet.
20. Through Shizuku's direct route. Shizuku's helper service never starts on this tablet, and since 0.9.0 commands don't need it (issue #4).

### Where each column comes from

- **Note Air6 C:** The maintainer's tablet, on firmware `4.3-rel_0817` (Android 16). Every check is recorded in [`knowledge/experiments.md`](knowledge/experiments.md).
- **Note Air4 C:** Its owner's reports in [issue #4](https://github.com/huuunleashed/BooxUltimatum/issues/4) (Shizuku tweaks, on 0.11.0) and [issue #5](https://github.com/huuunleashed/BooxUltimatum/issues/5) (the sleep screen).
- **Go 10.3:** Its owner's reports in [issue #4](https://github.com/huuunleashed/BooxUltimatum/issues/4) (Shizuku tweaks, on 0.9.1), with *Test Shizuku* results from two Shizuku builds.
- **Go 10.3 Gen II Lumi:** Its owner's reports in [issue #2](https://github.com/huuunleashed/BooxUltimatum/issues/2) (Instant ink) and [issue #3](https://github.com/huuunleashed/BooxUltimatum/issues/3) (the home screen), and in the [r/Onyx_Boox thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/Onyx_Boox/comments/1wqqsfz/) linked from them.
- **Note Air 2 Plus:** Its owner's report in [issue #1](https://github.com/huuunleashed/BooxUltimatum/issues/1) (Instant ink, on 0.6). The same owner has the Lumi.
- **NoteX3 Pro:** Its owner's report in [issue #6](https://github.com/huuunleashed/BooxUltimatum/issues/6), which brought the Simplified Chinese translation.
- **Not a Boox tablet:** The maintainer's Android 16 emulator, shaped like a Note Air6 C but without Boox firmware, and an owner's report of BooxUltimatum home on a Bigme B7 in the [r/Onyx_Boox thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/Onyx_Boox/comments/1wqqsfz/). BooxUltimatum hides what needs Boox firmware on such a tablet.

## Add your tablet

A report from a tablet that isn't here, or on a feature nobody has tried, is the most useful help right now. Open the [*Device finding* issue form](https://github.com/huuunleashed/BooxUltimatum/issues/new/choose), or use Device › Report a problem or idea in BooxUltimatum, which fills in the model, firmware and Android version for you. Say which features you tried and what happened, and whether the tablet was in portrait or landscape.

A report goes in this table as *Reported* or *Doesn't work*, with a note and a link to where it was made. It stays a report until the maintainer can check it on that tablet.

## Changing the table

The table is generated. Its data is in [`knowledge/devices.json`](knowledge/devices.json): the tablets, the features in their groups, and each cell's status and note. After changing it, run `python tools/dev/device_matrix.py`, which rewrites the table on this page and on the website (`site/index.html`). A check on every push fails when either is out of date.
