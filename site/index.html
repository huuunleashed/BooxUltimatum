BooxUltimatum

BooxUltimatum is an open-source suite of apps for e-ink Android tablets, tuned for the Boox Note Air. It gives you a calm home screen, sleep screens that stay current, Instant ink in other apps, a battery doctor and reversible tweaks. Nib, a separate drawing app, uses Boox's instant pen preview. None of it needs root.

Source on GitHub How it works

0.10 is out. The maintainer uses it every day on a Note Air6 C with firmware 4.3, where Instant ink works. On a Go 10.3 Lumi and a Note Air 2 Plus, testers reported that Instant ink had no effect, and those reports are open. Download the APK, and BooxUltimatum updates itself.

How much access it needs

Every feature asks for the least access it needs. The more ink a level has on the scale, the more it can reach. BooxUltimatum stays useful without the higher levels.

  1. T0 Just the app

    The home screen, the sleep and power-off screens, Instant ink, battery measurement, the tablet font, Settings (a searchable list of shortcuts to Android and Boox settings), Logs, and Nib, the drawing app. A few, such as Instant ink, need a switch you allow on the tablet itself.

    Useful from the first open

  2. T1 adb grants

    Four permissions you grant once from a computer with adb, Android's debugging tool, over USB. They stay in place after a restart. They add battery statistics inside the app, status bar icons shown or hidden across the tablet, and the tweaks that change Android settings.

  3. T2 Shizuku

    Shizuku is a free app that runs a small helper with the same rights as adb. Once it runs, BooxUltimatum needs no computer. It stops when the tablet restarts, and you start it again from a computer. It adds most tweaks, one-tap background fixes and the Wi-Fi network name in the home screen's header.

  4. T3 Root

    Not needed. BooxUltimatum works without root and doesn't ask for it. The Note Air6 C it was developed on has a locked bootloader.

Why no root, and what each tier can reach

Why it exists

The Note Air6 C is a lovely tablet with a frustrating side. Independent reviews such as eWritable's praise the screen and the pen. They also point at the two things owners complain about: battery life, and software that scatters useful settings across a dozen places.

BooxUltimatum is one owner's attempt to fix that from the inside. BooxUltimatum itself is one APK, and the apps that build on it, starting with Nib, install on their own. Nothing is flashed, and the bootloader stays locked.

What it does

How each part works

A home screen made for e-ink

BooxUltimatum home is a calm, paper-white home app. It needs no special access, and Android asks you to confirm before it becomes your home app. Measured on a Note Air6 C, it uses about a third of the Boox home's memory and no CPU when idle.

  • Widgets: clock, month, agenda, weather, battery, next alarm, a note, a Boox shelf (Library, Notes and Storage), and any app's widget, including Boox's own.
  • Apps: paged grids you turn with a swipe, folders, and icon shapes that remove the frame Boox draws into its own icons. Apps that Boox froze stay visible, dimmed with a frozen tag, instead of vanishing. With Shizuku, tapping one unfreezes and opens it. BooxUltimatum freezes it again about a minute after you return to the home screen, or when the tablet locks. Without Shizuku, turn the app on in Boox's App Freeze page first.
  • All apps also lists the Boox functions that have no app icon of their own, such as Notes, Library, Storage, Shop and Boox settings.
  • Go back to Boox is one tap away, and BooxUltimatum records the Boox home before you first switch.
The resource budget, measured

Your own sleep screen

Boox gives you six preset screensavers. BooxUltimatum gives you twenty-one faces, which are sleep screen designs. They are set in your tablet font, and each has its own portrait and landscape layout. Twelve of them can keep time while the tablet sleeps.

  • Live faces: Dial, Clock, Monitor, Cube (a 3D cube clock), Split-flap, Dashboard, Word clock, Day ring, Timeline, LCD, Sky and Broadsheet. Still faces: Almanac, Year, Instrument, Poster, Under the clock, Photo, Note, Return card and Minimal. Many have options of their own.
  • Kept current: Boox reads the picture only as the tablet goes to sleep, so BooxUltimatum redraws it while you use the tablet. Without updates while asleep, a clock face says when you put the tablet down, for example “put down around 4:30 PM”.
  • Both orientations are kept ready, so turning the tablet never leaves a cropped face.
  • While asleep (off until you turn it on): the face can update every 1 to 60 minutes while the tablet sleeps, and it covers the bar Boox draws while charging. It needs an accessibility service that only holds the picture and can't read the screen, plus background use and exact alarms, all allowed on the tablet. The 1-minute step runs only while charging, so on battery the shortest step is 5 minutes. Measured on a Note Air6 C, the 1-minute step cost about 1.6 mAh an hour.
How the picture reaches the sleep screen

Instant ink for other apps Experimental

In Boox Notes, ink appears under the nib in about 10 ms. In Sketchbook and most other apps, it trails behind. The display system can draw a stroke straight from the pen. This firmware feature is open to app developers through Onyx's public pen SDK, and most apps don't use it. BooxUltimatum turns the same feature on from the outside, for the apps you choose.

  • A preview stroke appears at once. After a pause you choose, the app's own brush takes its place. Instant ink keeps a pen session ready, so even the first stroke after unlocking gets the preview. Quick strokes share one preview, as in Boox Notes.
  • The app isn't modified in any way. Instant ink needs no root. It needs only usage access, which you allow on the tablet so BooxUltimatum knows which app is in front. It needs no Shizuku either, unless the display doesn't answer BooxUltimatum directly, and then the Ink page asks you to start it. If Android won't let you allow usage access, the Ink page shows the command to run from a computer. A Quick Settings tile turns Instant ink on and off.
  • Still being tuned: the preview is black only. Checked on a Note Air6 C with a real pen in Sketchbook. On a Go 10.3 Lumi and a Note Air 2 Plus, testers found it had no effect, and the Ink page says why. The Ink page also shows what it found, and has a pen test.
What the display system is asked to do

Nib, for drawing App of its own

Nib draws the way Boox Notes writes. The display paints each stroke under the nib at once, and Nib's own rendering takes its place the moment you lift. Nib is a separate app. BooxUltimatum's Suite page installs and updates it, and Nib works without BooxUltimatum, Shizuku or a computer.

  • Pens, pencils, markers, brushes and textured charcoal, each previewed in the closest of the display's own styles, and widths down to a fraction of a millimetre.
  • Unlimited layers, where Boox Notes stops at five. Undo, autosave that survives a crash, and zoom where a stroke is the same stroke at every zoom. The preview matches the result.
  • Tried with a real pen on a Note Air6 C: the preview feels clearly faster than in other drawing apps, and it stays off Nib's floating cards. Some of the display's preview styles aren't verified yet, so those brushes preview in the closest style that is.
How Nib is built

A battery doctor that doesn't drain the battery

BooxUltimatum tells you whether your battery is behaving, shows what used the charge, and keeps a log without ever waking the tablet to write a line. Which app used the charge needs usage access, and wake locks and wakeups need the adb grants or Shizuku.

  • Today: Normal, Watch or Problem (or Not enough data yet), with the numbers behind it, the time left at your usual use, and the last 24 hours as a strip. It needs at least an hour of recording to judge, and about a day of unplugged use to estimate the time left.
  • Battery log: records every screen, plug and level change, with the display's state and the app in front. You can share a battery report as one zip.
  • Found so far: on a Note Air6 C, standby is already excellent at 0 to 12 mA, and screen-on use is where the battery goes. One night the display stayed on while the tablet slept, and the log now notices when that happens.
How the log stays out of the way

Tweaks you can undo

Nineteen reversible tweaks cover apps, sleep, power, radios, the interface and privacy. Every tweak records what it replaced, so Undo puts it back. Each one shows the access it needs.

  • Let your apps keep running. Boox background-restricts every app you install, which is why music stops a minute after you leave the player. This tweak lifts the restriction.
  • Let Boox apps sleep takes non-essential Boox apps off the list of apps that Doze never pauses. Turn on Doze turns on Doze, Android's deep sleep, which the firmware turns off (checked on a Note Air6 C). Pause idle Boox apps stops Boox apps such as the store and the shop from running in the background. These three tweaks, and the one above, need Shizuku.
  • The whole tablet's font, at a proper weight, from a Google Fonts browser of 1946 families. It works without Shizuku, and Restore previous font undoes it.

Releases

From a release

Releases are on the Releases page as release-signed APKs. 0.10 follows a night when the display stayed on while the tablet slept. It adds a safeguard, Switch off a display that stays on, that keeps trying until the display is off. It also adds quiet hours you choose, a Battery page that no longer mistakes a refresh for a display that stayed on, a note when Android restarts by itself, and frozen apps that open and freeze again. 0.10.1 follows the next night the display stayed on. The safeguard is now on by default. Refresh the sleep screen while asleep asks the sleep screen to refresh whenever 30 minutes pass without one while the tablet sleeps. The note after Android restarts by itself stays until you restart the tablet. 0.10.2 follows research into what Android restarting by itself leaves behind. Battery now says within about 30 minutes when the tablet's processor stayed awake through a sleep. It no longer reports that as a display that stayed on, and the two safeguards have plainer names. 0.11.0 is a checkpoint for the words of the suite. Every description in the apps and every document follows one guide, with one name for each thing, and what the maintainer measured on one tablet now says which. 0.12.0 gives the suite its own language. The app follows the tablet’s language until you choose another one, in a Language row of its own, and on Android 13 and later the app’s languages also appear in Android’s own App languages screen. The hub, Nib, the shared kit and the updater read in Simplified Chinese and Vietnamese, and a check holds every translation to the same rules, so a new language is a folder and a line. No dates are promised. Each release ships when it works on the maintainer's tablet.

From source, today

# JDK 21 and the Android SDK (platform 36)
.\gradlew.bat assembleRelease
adb install -r hub\build\outputs\apk\release\hub-release.apk

The last line uses adb, Android's debugging tool, to install the APK from a computer. Without a signing key, the build is debug-signed. That is fine for a tablet that has no BooxUltimatum yet. A tablet that already runs the published release needs a build signed with the same key, or Android refuses the update. The README has the steps, and how to go back to the Boox home.

Not affiliated with Onyx or BOOX. BooxUltimatum is an independent project, not endorsed or supported by Onyx International. “BOOX”, “Note Air” and related names are trademarks of their owners, used here only to say which tablet this is for.

BooxUltimatum changes system settings. If you ask it to, it also changes the home screen, the sleep screen and how other apps draw. Every change is recorded and reversible, and nothing touches the system partitions or the bootloader. Still, this is pre-release software that relies on unofficial behaviour, so use it at your own risk. As the GPL says, it comes with no warranty.
